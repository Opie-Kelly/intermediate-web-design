Movie Review: Mr. Forgettable

#Note: Not A Real Movie.

Story / Plot

The story follows Arin Walten, a man who feels unseen and unheard by the world. He believes the world should just forget about him, and wishes for that very thing. The wish he makes ends up coming true, and nobody is able to remember him. While at first he is happy with the wish coming true, he soon finds out that being forgotten by the world isn't as good as he imagined it would be.

Opinion

As for how good the movie was, I have to say I quite enjoyed it. The story is very interesting, and makes good use of the concept of being forgotten by everyone. The movie does a good job at tugging at your heart strings, but doesn't try to keep the story to sad. While the cinematography doesn't stand out that much, the sound design and music used in the movie is a different story. The background music in many of the scenes helps so much with the movies expression of emotion, and doesn't ever ruin the feeling of the movie. All in all, the story of Mr. Forgettable is anything but, and I would highly recommend seeing this movie if you haven't yet.

Other Notes

The concept of the world forgetting you exist is an interesting idea, with a lot of potential. This same concept can work in a movie of a different genre than this one, like a sci-fi or horror movie. Unique movies like this are always a breath of fresh air from the similar stories we see all the time nowadays. It's especially nice to see movies like this do well, as it gives other directors the confidence to make more unquie movies, rather than stay in thier comfort zone.
